TLDR: v0.8.1 is about daily usability. Ember is more honest with you, smarter about when to reach for the web, and the things that were broken — the stop button, the mobile header, the installer updates, the reminder that vanished — are fixed.

Character and Behavior

Anti-sycophancy: Ember doesn't cave now. She held positions before, but had a pattern of softening when pushed. That's been addressed in both the behavior rules and her character document. She'll update her view when you give her a good reason. Not because you pushed back.
Register: Ember was slipping into a therapeutic register on emotional topics — reframing, reflecting, gently redirecting. The coaching filter's been extended to catch more of those patterns before they reach you.

Web Search

Ask-first classifier: Ember now routes queries through a three-stage intent classifier before deciding whether to search. She asks when there's real uncertainty, searches autonomously when the intent is clear.

Tasks and Reminders

Time-qualified reminders work: "Remind me at 5pm to stop working" now creates the task. The previous bug broke silently on any phrase between "remind me" and "to." Fixed.

Mobile

Header collapse: The header fits on a single row at 360px. Vault badge, model name, and feature icons hide at mobile width. Bare mode, vault toggle, and export move into a ⋯ overflow menu. Desktop is unchanged.

Controls and Updates

Shut Down Ember works: The button now calls a real endpoint and cleanly stops the API. Previous behavior: 404, swallowed silently, nothing happened.
Installer updates everything: The update flow now pulls all three repos. Previously only the backend updated and the UI stayed behind. Also fixed: dirty package-lock.json was silently aborting UI pulls.
